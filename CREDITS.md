## Approximations
Any geometry not backed by data (for example a tunnel cross-section without measurements) is listed here with what it is based on.

- **Seabed**: the MDT holds 0.0 m over water, so sea cells inside the CV05 `Mar Mediterrani` polygon are set to a constant −3.0 m (`seaFill` in `dem.json` and `collision.json`). It is a placeholder under the sea surface, not a bathymetry. Land heights are never altered.
- **Tunnel floor heights** (approximate, spec Q2/Q6): the DEM over and beside the tunnel is rock, not floor (at the west portal it reads 182.87 m, 4-11 m above the footpath), so the floor is curated in `data/curated/tunnel.toml` and runs linearly in path distance between the portals: west 171.0 m (range 170.0-172.5), east 193.0 m (192.3-193.7), a climb of 22 m over the 49 m path (about 24 degrees). West basis: the footpath before the steps is at 168-170 m in the DEM and the ground LiDAR, plus 6 steps (OSM `step_count`) of an assumed 0.18 m, about 169.9 m; the lowest LiDAR return within 3 m outside the portal is 172.1 m. East basis: the DEM at the portal (193.12 m) agrees with the LiDAR along the first 5 m of the path leaving it (medians 192.3-192.7 m). No height was measured on site; confirm with the park or a visit. `penyal tunnel --analyse` recomputes the evidence and a test fails if the file drifts from the data.
- **Tunnel cross-section** (placeholder, spec Q2): 2.0 m wide and 2.6 m high (ranges 1.5-2.5 m and 2.2-3.2 m), a flat floor under vertical walls and a flattened vault. No dimensions are published and the three openly licensed Wikimedia Commons photos reviewed (Spotter2, CC BY-SA 3.0 / GFDL; chisloup, CC BY 3.0; Josemanuel, CC BY-SA 2.5) contain no person or object of known size, so they only support a section about one to two people wide. The photos are not stored in the repository and not used as textures; the interior is plain grey. Replace with measurements or a phone-LiDAR scan.
- **Terrain at the tunnel portals**: the 1 m DEM is the rock wall at the west portal, so the terrain tile `t_2_-4` (and the collision heightfield) is lowered by up to 13.3 m along the last 8.7 m of the steps, in a 6 m wide strip, down to the footpath floor, ending at the portal plane (39 DEM cells change). The tunnel volume (plus 1 m beyond each portal) is cut out of the surface in LOD0 and LOD1 of that tile so the mouths are visible; LOD2 keeps the rock. This is geometry, not data: it follows the approximate floor above.
- **West approach path height**: the footpath along the OSM steps way follows the DEM for its first 8 m, then climbs in a straight line to the curated west floor; the first 5 m of the path leaving the east portal fade from the east floor into the DEM.
- **Corridor shape**: the walkable corridor is the OSM trail buffered by 3 m plus discs of 15 / 10 / 8 / 6 m (forecourt, summit, Mirador de Carabiners, viewpoints), as decided in ADR 0006. Inside the tunnel it is the placeholder width minus 0.3 m (player capsule radius) on each side. The forecourt disc is centred on the point of the Centre d'Informació footprint nearest to the trail.
- **Pine shape** (generated, not measured): `blender/pine.py` invents a curved trunk, layered branches and needle tufts after the open crown of an Aleppo pine, in 4 seeded variants. Only the position, height and crown radius of each tree come from the 2016 LiDAR (`vegetation.json`); the species is not verified for every crown (`docs/specs/002/vegetacion-especies.md`). The 3–10 m crowns are assumed to be mostly Aleppo pine, which fits the orthophoto but is not confirmed with reference photos. Replace the shape when photos or a scan of real trees arrive.
- **Foliage colour** (`pipeline/src/penyal_pipeline/foliage_tint.py`): the needles of the pines and the leaves of the lentisc, rosemary and grass are the untouched Poly Haven textures multiplied by a per-vertex colour measured on hand-chosen boxes of two reference photographs (Wikimedia Commons `26-Le-sentier-du-penon-de-ifach` and `17-Calpe-panoramio-68`): the sunlit median of each foliage, kept in hue and saturation (the pine's olive 62 degrees / 0.48; the lentisc, rosemary and grass boxes are dry-season brown-olive). A multiplier cannot brighten, so the pine keeps 0.44 and the rosemary 0.68 of the photographs' brightness. The rosemary estimate mixes a low shrub, soil and a dark shrub and is the weakest.
- **Crown radius and presence** (`vegetation.json`): the radius is estimated from the 1 m canopy height model and is approximate (LiDAR density is 0.5–1 point/m²). Crowns are dropped when their 2024 orthophoto NDVI (band 4 as near infrared, identified from the data) is under 0.12, the median of bare ground measured with the same estimator; the thresholds come from measured reference classes, not from ground truth.
- **Understory** (`vegetation.json`): shrubs and grass are placed beside a random 15 % of the LiDAR low and medium vegetation points (so they clump where the vegetation does). The three Poly Haven models are generic stand-ins (lentisc, rosemary or lavender, scrub grass); no protected species is represented or located. **Cliff vegetation is deliberately not placed**: no understory goes on slopes of 50 degrees or more between 60 and 385 m a.s.l., the elevation band of the fissure habitat of *Silene hifacensis*, so the world cannot be read as a map of where such plants grow; tree crowns on slopes of 60 degrees or more are kept only when wider than the minimum radius and green (NDVI 0.24 or more), which removes LiDAR/DEM artefacts on the walls. Clearances around OSM paths, roads and buildings are provisional until the path-surface widths of E1 replace them.
- **Limestone stand-in**: the Penyal is Eocene nummulitic limestone; the rock detail comes from the Poly Haven `marble_cliff_05` texture, a visual stand-in (not geology), tinted by the orthophoto's local colour so the hue stays that of the real rock. When the textures are not loaded, a procedural noise detail is used instead; neither is surveyed geometry or photography of the Penyal.
- **Path surface widths** (provisional, spec 003 E1.1): OSM has no widths for the ways painted into the surface masks (`penyal surfaces`), so `HIGHWAY_WIDTHS` in `pipeline/src/penyal_pipeline/surfaces.py` assumes one width per `highway` value: footway 2.0 m, path 1.2 m, steps 1.5 m, pedestrian 4.0 m, service / track 3.0 m, residential 6.0 m (a `width` tag in OSM would win). They are approximations, not measurements, and are recorded as `widths.status = "provisional"` in `surfaces/manifest.json`; replace them with the tape measurements of `docs/specs/002/protocolo-fotos.md` ("Fotos y medidas para modelar") and rerun the command. Ways without a `surface` tag, with `rock` or `concrete` paint nothing, and `dirt` / `ground` / `gravel` / `compacted` share one class and `sett` / `paving_stones` another because nothing distinguishes them in the data.
- **Staircases** (provisional, spec 003 E1.4): `penyal structures` models the OSM staircases `way/26330269` (30 steps), `way/862108205` (10) and `way/862108208` (6, the steps to the west portal) from their `step_count`. The riser is uniform and derived (rise / count); the treads follow the carved DEM as the tiles render it: each nosing sits where the monotone envelope of the highest ground across the flight's 1.5 m width reaches `base + k * riser`, so tread depth varies and the ground never rises above a tread (worst clearance on the carved DEM -6 mm, 0 mm and 0 mm, within the 2 cm tolerance of 10 cm sampling of a bilinear DEM). A flight starts where the ground beside it stops overhanging the axis by more than 10 cm, and ends where that ground peaks plus one nominal tread, standing at most 15 cm above the axis DEM there. Derived, not measured (real start and end along the way, offset of the ends above the axis DEM, riser, mean tread with its range): `centre-steps` 0.0-16.94 m, +0.02/+0.08 m, 0.166 m, 0.565 m (0.31-1.44 m); `steps-10` 0.0-9.0 m of its 11.32 m, +0.02/+0.12 m, 0.224 m (0.209 m on the axis alone), 0.90 m (0.44-2.23 m, the last tread is the flat top); `portal-steps` 9.15-15.93 m of its 16.69 m, +0.06/0.00 m, 0.139 m, 1.13 m (1.08-1.37 m). The portal flight starts after the curated 8.0 m approach, once the rock wall stops overhanging (about 9 m), and stops 0.75 m before the portal plane, where the carve strip ends and its last half metre blends back into the rock. Its riser of 0.139 m is NOT evidence against the 0.18 m assumed in `data/curated/tunnel.toml`: it is derived from a carved DEM that itself derives from the curated 171.0 m west floor, so the comparison is circular; only the user's measurement can settle it. Assumed, not measured, and replaced by the tape measurements of the photo protocol: width 1.5 m, handrail axis 0.9 m above the nosing line with 5 cm square bars and posts about every 1.5 m, 10 cm inside the edge. **Handrail assumptions:** `handrail=yes` names no side, so one rail on the right-hand side going up is modelled (the least the tag guarantees; two rails or the other side may be the truth), and it is modelled as dark metal although the reference photos (`docs/research/003-fotos-referencia.md`) show wooden two-rail fences along the trail, so the real handrail material is unknown. `way/862108205` is tagged `incline=down` but the DEM rises 2.2 m along the way, so the flight is oriented by the DEM. The portal flight is measured along the polyline the carve uses (`trail.segment_polyline_2d`, asserted equal to the way's node order). Materials are flat PBR colours (no downloaded texture). The steps are buried 25 cm below the lowest ground under each tread so nothing floats. The collision proxy is one horizontal quad per tread; every riser stays under the 0.4 m step height of the player.
- **Visitor-centre buildings** (approximate, spec 003 E3): `penyal buildings` extrudes the OSM footprints `way/26330271` (Centro de Interpretación, 651 m2) and `way/26330272` (the building beside it, 483 m2). Facades, colours and roof type come from ONE photo of 2014 (Commons, Panoramio, `docs/research/003-fotos-referencia.md` photo 1); dimensions come from the OSM footprint and the 2016 ICV LiDAR (class 6) in absolute elevation. Walls start at the lowest DEM ground under the footprint, with a stone band 0.5 m over the local ground. Eave = 25th and ridge = 95th percentile of the roof points (centre 74.02 m / 76.11 m, second building 76.84 m / 79.79 m), one hip roof with a flat top (scale 0.4 of the footprint) and a 0.5 m overhang. The model follows the LiDAR roof with an RMS error of 1.5 m (centre) and 1.6 m (second building) because the real roofs fall about 2 m across each building and use several levels; terraces and the solar-panel area are NOT modelled. One turret is modelled on the centre building (a LiDAR local maximum 2 m over the roof, 5 m square at the clamp, 3 m tall over the roof model); the second building has none in the LiDAR. NOT modelled: the porch of semicircular arches, doors, windows, chimneys, roof tiles in relief, collision (the player can walk through the walls). The tiled textures are generic Poly Haven materials, tinted towards the photo colours (whitewash lifted to a brightest channel of 215 because the photo is underexposed), not the real surfaces.
