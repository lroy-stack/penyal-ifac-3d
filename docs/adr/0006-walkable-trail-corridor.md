# 0006. Limit walking to the trail corridor and viewpoints

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-11
- Deciders: lr0y (product owner)

## Context
The park rules forbid leaving the path. The rock has near-vertical cliffs where airborne LiDAR is sparse, and detail budget on mid-range phones is limited. The experience is meant to feel like walking the real trail.

## Options considered
1. **Corridor = buffer around the official trail (OSM ways of the Ruta Roja) plus viewpoint areas** — teaches the real rule, concentrates detail where people walk, avoids exposing low-quality cliff geometry up close.
2. **Free roaming over the whole rock** — more freedom, but contradicts the rules, needs detail everywhere, and lets players stand on cliffs that cannot be represented faithfully.

## Decision
Option 1, approved by the product owner on 2026-10-11. The corridor polygon is generated by the pipeline (`build/trail.json`) and enforced by the player controller with a soft clamp and a short message explaining the rule.

## Consequences
- Terrain detail and vegetation density are highest inside and near the corridor.
- Changing to free roaming later means raising detail across the rock and revisiting collision for cliffs.
